THE FUTURE I WANT TO HELP BUILD

Personal aspirations and long-term engineering philosophy — distinct from completed academic and software work.

THESIS // SYMBIOSIS

Technological Capability Without Ecological Exhaustion

The long-term ambition behind my engineering choices is straightforward: helping humanity advance technologically while keeping Earth habitable. Too often, industrial capability and living systems are treated as a zero-sum trade-off. I want to help build the opposite — symbiosis.

In practice, that starts at the physical level: understanding energy, materials, heat, wear, and waste in real engineering systems, and building computational models that make machines and manufacturing lines drastically more resource-intelligent.

PYNAEA // INDUSTRIAL RESOURCE INTELLIGENCE

Symbiosis is also the founding philosophy behind Pynaea — focused on Industrial Resource Intelligence, beginning with manufacturing systems and aiming toward broader resource intelligence across energy and material flows.
