Physics-Informed Fourier Neural Operators for Localized 2D Atmospheric Transport Nowcasting

First-Author Manuscript · Double-Blind Submission (Co-authors omitted for review compliance)

2026

Abstract Summary: Autoregressive neural operators trained purely on data loss often suffer from non-physical dissipation and accumulated drift over long rollout horizons. We present a Physics-Informed Fourier Neural Operator (PI-FNO) architecture for localized 2D advection-diffusion-decay transport systems. By penalizing a discrete finite-difference PDE residual directly within the Fourier training loop, the model constrains physical consistency across multi-step rollout horizons.

Across controlled data scaling from 64 to 1024 trajectories, evaluation MSE decreases by 79.4% and Relative \(L_2\) error drops by 52.1%. At \(\lambda_{\text{phy}} = 0.01\), physics regularization yields a 36.1% reduction in PDE residual with only a 2.7% delta in prediction MSE. On a 1,000-trajectory evaluation, PI-FNO demonstrates a 7.5% lower prediction MSE and 39.5% lower PDE residual relative to an unregularized baseline.
