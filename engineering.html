METHODOLOGY

The Engineering Approach

Every engineering problem I tackle follows a disciplined progression—from fundamental governing physics down to verified, robust computational execution.

01

Understand the Physics

Identify the active physical domains: thermodynamics, fluid flow, structural mechanics, heat transport, or dynamic contact. Establish what physical interactions dominate and what assumptions (laminar, incompressibility, small-strain elasticity) are physically valid.

02

Formulate the Problem

Derive the governing differential equations: Navier–Stokes for fluid momentum, advection-diffusion for energy, biharmonic Airy stress equations for elasticity, or Archard kinetics for surface wear. Specify rigorous Dirichlet, Neumann, and Robin boundary conditions.

03

Build the Model

Construct the geometric representation and spatial discretization: CAD boundaries, finite volume structured grids, finite element meshes, or continuous implicit distance fields. Formulate discrete conservation stencils preserving mass, momentum, and energy.

04

Compute

Implement numerical solvers directly using high-performance languages: C++, CUDA GPU parallelism, sparse matrix libraries (cuSPARSE), and memory-efficient algorithms. Ensure compute efficiency and parallel scalability without unnecessary memory allocations.

05

Improve

Apply gradient-based topology optimization, adjoint sensitivity methods, or physics-regularized scientific ML to accelerate convergence, minimize thermal compliance, or discover optimal material configurations.

06

Validate

Verify numerical convergence, grid independence, analytical solution parity, and benchmark baselines. Quantify PDE residuals and compare against trusted solvers to ensure the computational result reflects true physical behaviour.
